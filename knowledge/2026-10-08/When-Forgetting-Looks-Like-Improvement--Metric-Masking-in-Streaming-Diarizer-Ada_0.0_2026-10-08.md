## 项目描述
arXiv:2610.08828v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Small-data adaptation can improve speech detection while degrading speaker attribution. We study this discrepancy in a released streaming diarizer adapted on 7.5 h of two-party conversation and evaluated across six corpora. Adaptation substantially improves in-domain diarization performance and transfers to an independent corpus. However, this improvement is not consistent across evaluation scenarios as the additional confusion is mainly associated with impaired temporal identity consistency rather than speaker-count errors. A local-remapping diagnostic reveals different patterns of identity degradation across corpora, indicating that adaptation may alter how streaming models maintain speaker assignments over time. Rehearsal reduces the observed degradation but reduces the cross-domain transfer performance. These results highlight the need to jointly evaluate detection accuracy, identity consistency, and retention behavior when adapting streaming diarization systems.
