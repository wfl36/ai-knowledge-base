## 项目描述
arXiv:2611.33173v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: The Bundesrat is Germany's second legislative chamber and a constitutional veto player within its political system. The members of the Bundesrat are delegates of state governments. Tracing the political discourse in the Bundesrat is challenging as it has a limited machine-readable corpus available. To address this issue, we present a speaker-attributed corpus of 1,067 Bundesrat plenary sessions from the chamber's first session on 7 September 1949 to 10 July 2026. In this period, a total of 53,362 speeches by 2,473 individuals were delivered in the chamber, including written statements. Each speech includes a speech ID, the full German text, the speaker's name, the date and location of the session, the federal state that the speaker represents, the speaker's role (if applicable), the type of contribution, and the speaker's gender. The dataset provides interdisciplinary research opportunities across computational political science, legislative studies, and parliamentary discourse analysis, coupled with discourse from the Bundestag.
