Hey HN!

Darwin stands out because it&#x27;s designed for developers who want to leverage AI without needing deep expertise in LLMs or prompt engineering. It offers:<p>- hands-off approach to automate routine development tasks.<p>- Novel and creative ways of making LLMs work for you<p>- A unique API for each project, allowing for customized automation tools.<p>Currently, Darwin is in alpha.
