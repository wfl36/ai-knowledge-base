## 项目描述
arXiv:2610.20631v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Romance scam has become a serious problem via social media, and the improvement of artificial intelligence enable to fake identify easily. In the study, a chatbot has developed to detect romance scam in real-time through Line Messaging API. Using the seven stage theories in the social science field, the stages of romance scam can be detected through large language model. In addition, once a romance scam is detected, the chatbot provides explanations of the stage and prevention advices through large language model. The contribution of the work provides a practical application, and can be used in real scenario for reducing the risk of romance scam.
