## 项目描述
arXiv:2608.28619v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Medical history taking is a dialogue-based clinical reasoning task in which learners must gather, organise, and integrate patient information while the consultation unfolds. Generative AI-powered virtual patients (GenAI VPs) make repeated history taking practice scalable and preserve full turn by turn dialogue. However, these logs are educationally difficult to use directly. Complete transcripts are too detailed for routine teacher review, whereas final scores obscure whether learners followed up patient cues, checked uncertainty, or used summaries to guide later questioning. This study examined whether coded GenAI VP dialogues can provide teacher-interpretable process evidence of clinical reasoning. We analysed 1{,}030 GenAI VP dialogues from 210 second-year medical learners across five weeks chest-pain cases. Each consultation was teacher-scored using a rubric assessing the full history taking dialogue, and consultations were classified within each week as high- or low-rated using the weekly median score. To explain how rated performance was reflected in the dialogue process, we applied three analytic layers to the same coded dialogue data: behavioural prevalence, local co-occurrence using Epistemic Network Analysis, and sequential transition using Transition Network Analysis. High-rated consultations involved more history taking activity, but differences were not simply about volume. High rated consultations more often connected information gathering and symptom exploration with communication, checking, organisation, and synthesis. Summarising and organising moves more often led to verification or mechanism-oriented follow-up. These findings show how layered analysis of GenAI VP dialogue logs can reveal process patterns associated with high rated history taking and support process-focused feedback in medical education.

## 综合总结
本文研究了如何利用生成式AI虚拟病人(GenAI VPs)的对话日志为医学教师提供可解释的过程性评价证据。研究基于210名二年级医学生5周共1030次胸痛病例对话，采用行为频率分析、Epistemic Network Analysis共现分析和Transition Network Analysis序列转移分析三层方法，发现高评分对话在信息收集、症状探索与沟通、检查、组织、整合等行为间形成更紧密的关联网络，且总结和组织行为更常导向验证或机制导向的后续提问。研究展示了将GenAI对话日志转化为过程性反馈证据的可行路径。

## 分析摘要
### 技术先进性 (评分: 5.5/10)
研究将GenAI虚拟病人对话日志通过三层分析方法（行为频率、Epistemic Network Analysis共现分析、Transition Network Analysis序列转移分析）转化为教师可解释的过程性证据。方法论上属于学习分析(Learning Analytics)领域，融合了对话分析与教育数据挖掘，但技术创新性有限，所用分析工具(ENA/TNA)均为既有方法，在AI核心技术层面缺乏方法论突破。数据规模(1030次对话,210名学员)适中，分析框架合理但深度一般。

### 实用性 (评分: 6.0/10)
对医学教育从业者具有明确的实践参考价值：展示了如何将非结构化的AI对话日志转化为可操作的过程性评估证据，可直接指导医学教师利用GenAI虚拟病人进行形成性评价与反馈。适用范围聚焦于病史采集这一特定临床推理任务，对其他对话型临床训练的可迁移性尚需验证。对教育技术开发者和学习设计师也提供了可借鉴的对话日志分析方法论。
