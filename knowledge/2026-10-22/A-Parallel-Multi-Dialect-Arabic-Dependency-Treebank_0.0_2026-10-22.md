## 项目描述
arXiv:2610.28823v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Dependency parsing resource development for Arabic has largely focused on Modern Standard Arabic, leaving dialectal Arabic comparatively under-resourced. We introduce MADARTB, the first large-scale parallel dependency treebank spanning five city dialects: Beirut, Cairo, Doha, Rabat and Tunis, and extending an existing parallel MSA version. In total MADARTB consists of 5,000 sentences annotated using the CATiB dependency scheme. We analyze syntactic similarity across all city dialects and MSA. Although global construction profiles exhibit high similarity across varieties, local syntactic structures frequently diverge, revealing substantial structural variation across dialects. We further establish a cross-dialect dependency parsing comparison using a supervised parser and four LLMs. The supervised parser achieves 87.5% LAS, while the best LLM setup trails at 85.7% LAS across all six varieties on the test set. We release our annotations and models publicly.
