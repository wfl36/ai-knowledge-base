## 项目描述
arXiv:2611.33195v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Materials discovery is an iterative loop: experiments and calculations verify predictions, and each outcome can inform the next. Yet most predictors are specialized single-task models fixed after training. Large language models have recently emerged as predictors across heterogeneous tasks but generally underperform specialized models, and retrieval-augmented variants discard their own outcomes. Benchmarks also score queries in isolation, leaving improvement from outcomes untested. Here we construct MatMemBench, a sequential benchmark of 22 materials tasks that reveals outcomes only after prediction and tests online adaptation, memory retention and cross-task transfer. We present MatRecall, a framework that lets large language models store verified predictions, with evidence, outcome and error, as episodes and recall them without retraining. MatRecall improved median performance by 62% over a memory-free model, exceeded specialized models on 11 tasks, remained useful after feedback stopped and transferred between related tasks. Verified experience could become a reusable resource for self-improving discovery loops.
