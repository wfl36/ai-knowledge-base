## 项目描述
arXiv:2611.17464v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: In an era where linguistic creativity is challenged by algorithmic authorship, we pose a provocative question: can artificial intelligence (AI) write an infinite poem? We set up two experiments to investigate the quantitative and qualitative output produced by AI utilizing couplets that evidence a poetic relationship between end words, the only variation in a strict anaphoric, litany structure. Our results show that while AI can generate interesting pairings, it still falls short of human-level creativity and understanding of poetic relationships. We offer commentary and a brief survey of the field of computational poetry experiments, tracing back authorial intention and distinctions of 'natural' and 'artificial' poetry, as well as justifying why couplet form is suited to testing AI's poetic output. Experiment 1 relies on manual prompting and co-creation to extend the original poem, which is developed into an animated webpage installation and interactive artwork. Experiment 2 reliably achieves automated output reaching the 1000 benchmark via an alternative method of using an LLM in combination with semantic similarity, of which Method 2 demonstrates a higher capacity for semantic and quantitative poetic output. These experiments highlight the limitations of current LLMs' generation capacity at the word level but also suggest that poetry will also respond and revive by incorporating AI workflows.
