## 项目描述
arXiv:2611.26903v1 Announce Type: new Abstract: Partial-edit deepfake localization aims to identify manipulated regions within otherwise authentic speech. Recent systems report strong overall results, but aggregate scores do not reveal whether this performance holds across different editing conditions. In this work, we present an edit- and generator-conditioned analysis of Segment-Aware Localization (SAL) using WavLM-Large and XLS-R representations on the VoiceCraft and SSR-Speech subsets of PartialEdit. We categorize edits along three transcript-derived axes, namely content, meaning change, and editing operation, and evaluate each generator separately. While both representations achieve strong overall performance, localization is noticeably weaker on SSR-Speech. From our experiments, we found that the deletion is the hardest operation, and deletions produced by SSR-Speech are the most challenging condition for both representations. In contrast, edit content, meaning change, and sentence novelty have smaller effects. These findings show that a single aggregate score can mask important weaknesses, and that partial-edit localization should be evaluated separately across generators and edit types.
